# LinkedIn Product Launch Campaign
## Making AI Evaluation a Part of Every Release

**Brand:** Plumloom  
**Content type:** LinkedIn organic launch post  
**Target audience:** AI engineers, ML engineers, product managers, and engineering leaders  
**Objective:** Build awareness and encourage developers to explore AI evaluation workflows.

---

### Main LinkedIn Post

Building an AI application is one challenge.

Knowing whether it is reliable enough to release is another.

AI applications and agents can produce impressive results during development, but how do you know whether they will continue to perform as expected when real users interact with them?

Without a consistent evaluation process, teams can struggle to identify:

- Inconsistent AI-generated responses
- Changes in output quality after updates
- Unexpected agent behaviour
- Reliability issues across different use cases
- Whether a new version is ready for release

**AI evaluation should not be an afterthought. It should be part of the development lifecycle.**

That's the problem Plumloom is designed to help engineering and product teams address.

Plumloom provides AI evaluation and reliability tools that help teams evaluate AI applications and agents, understand their performance, and make more confident release decisions.

Whether you're developing an AI-powered application or building agent-based workflows, having a structured evaluation process can help your team move forward with greater confidence.

The goal isn't simply to build AI faster.

It's to build AI that your team can evaluate, understand, and improve.

**Explore Plumloom and rethink how your team evaluates AI applications.**

1. Introduce a problem developers experience.
